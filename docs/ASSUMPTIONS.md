# Assumptions and Open Questions

- Iteration 2 uses OTT 3.3, the historical release cited by the prototype. A newer release and future update policy remain separate decisions.
- Taxonomy refresh, uncertainty presentation, formal accessibility targets, and production service levels are intentionally deferred.
- Define the displayed-subset rules from concrete taxonomy examples; only exclusion of unnamed taxa is confirmed now.
- Define rank semantics and visual encoding through comparative prototypes.
